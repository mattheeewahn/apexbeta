    Guardian Consent

    Your guardian needs to approve this

    You told us you're under 14. Korean law (PIPA) says a child under 14 needs their legal guardian's consent, so we'll email your guardian and ask for it.

    You don't have to wait for their answer to finish signing up. You have one month to get it — after that your account is deactivated until your guardian consents. Nothing is deleted, and their approval switches it straight back on.

    What APEX collects, and why

    Taken from our Privacy Policy — show this to your guardian too.

    What we collect

    • To make your account: your full name, username, school email, school, account type, date of birth, grade, and your password stored only as a one-way hash — we never store or see the password itself.
    • From your profile: profile picture, biography, resume entries, your privacy preferences, who you follow, and who you've blocked.
    • What you create: posts, Lounge and Direct Messages with their images and reactions, and reports you submit. Direct Messages are encrypted at rest.
    • From your device: only a push notification registration if you turn notifications on, performance diagnostics, and ordinary server request logs including IP address. There is no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDK.
    • As you use APEX: login sessions, timestamps on what you create, and your moderation history.

    Why we collect it

    • To run your account — sign you in, show your profile, and deliver your messages and posts.
    • To display you — your name, username, picture, school and grade appear on your profile and next to your content. Your email address is never shown publicly.
    • Your date of birth — to show a birthday indicator, which you can switch off, and to apply this age rule.
    • Safety — every post and message is screened by automated moderation before it is published, and Moderators can review reported content.

    We do not sell your information, do not share it with advertisers or data brokers, do not use it for advertising, and do not train our own models on your content. You or your guardian can ask us to delete your account and data at any time.

                    Frequently Asked Questions

                    Why does Windows say it "protected my PC"?

                    Windows shows that screen for any app that hasn't been code-signed yet. It doesn't mean anything is wrong with APEX. Click More info, then Run anyway, and you'll only see it once.

                    Why does my Mac say it can't check APEX?

                    Your Mac says that about any app that isn't from the App Store or registered with Apple yet. It doesn't mean anything is wrong with APEX. Click Done, open System Settings, go to Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to APEX, then confirm. You only have to do this once. On macOS 14 or older, you can instead Control-click APEX in Applications and choose Open.

                    My phone won't let me install the APK.

                    Android blocks apps from outside the Play Store until you allow them. When it asks, turn on Allow from this source for the browser you downloaded with. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap Install anyway.

                    Why can't I add APEX from Chrome on my iPhone?

                    Adding APEX to the Home Screen works best from Safari. Open apexsns.net in Safari and follow the steps above.

                    Do I need a new account?

                    No. Log in with the account you already use. Your posts, DMs, lounges and settings are all there.

                    How do I update the app?

                    You don't need to. The apps load the latest version of APEX every time you open them, so you always have the newest features.

                    Will I get notifications?

                    On Android, and on iPhone once APEX is on your Home Screen, you can turn notifications on from APEX's settings. The Windows and Mac apps show new messages while they're open but can't notify you while they're closed yet.

                    Can I use it on a school computer?

                    The Windows app doesn't need admin rights, but some school-managed computers block apps they don't recognize. If yours does, APEX works in any browser at apexsns.net.
